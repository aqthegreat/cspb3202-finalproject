Deliverables

- A video clip of a demo(s) - either .mp4 YouTube link, unlisted, or transformed to .gif image (you can leave those links in the report or embed in the notebook)

- Report with following contents (no page limit):
  - Short overview of what your project is about (e.g. you're building /testing certain RL models in certain environments; yes you can test your algorithm in more than 1 environment if your goal is to test an algorithm(s) performances in different settings)
  - Approach: Explain your environment, your choice of model(s), the methods and purpose of testing and experiments, explain any trouble shooting required.
  - Results: Show the results and interpretation of your experiment. Any iterative improvements summary.
  - Conclusion, discussion, reflection, or suggestions for future improvements or future ideas.
  - References: Please include all relevant links (git, video, etc)

Note that it's not about debugging or programming/implementation, but about when a correctly implemented algorithm wasn't enough for the problem and the author had to modify/add some features or techniques, or compare with another model, etc. (7)
- Does it include the results summary, interpretation of experiments and visualization (e.g. performance comparison table, graphs etc)? (7)

- Does it include all deliverables? (3)
  - Git with codes or notebooks
